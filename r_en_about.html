Our Story

Founded in 2008, we have grown from a small workshop into a leading OEM/ODM sportswear manufacturer serving global brands. Our 50,000 sqm facility houses advanced manufacturing equipment and a dedicated R&D team of 30+ professionals.

We specialize in producing high-performance activewear, yoga wear, running gear, team uniforms, and custom sportswear solutions for brands worldwide. Our commitment to quality and innovation has earned us long-term partnerships with clients in North America, Europe, and Australia.
